Engagement Models

Xove offers flexible and customized engagement models specific to customer needs covering multiple locations with 24X7 coverage. We ensure customer satisfaction at all levels in the organization. Transparency and commitment will be maintained throughout the engagement. Our focus is to maintain High Speed, High Quality and Low Cost for all the projects engaged. Xove’s unique Global resourcing helps to scale fast and deliver 24 hours.

Staff Aug (T&M)

This is one of our widely used pricing model where it mitigates the risks of ambiguity and frequent changes in project scope and timelines. Many clients engage with us through this model for projects that involve emerging technologies, undocumented Business Process or Workflow Applications, or dynamic requirements due evolving/emerging markets.

Our IT Recruitment team helps you to speed up and fill skill gap in order to take you to finish line. We fulfill resourcing needs right from Short – Term to Long Term projects, Project / Program Management, Quality Assurance and Cutting-Edge Next Gen Technologies/. Our Staffing Solution gives you the agility, focusing on the strategic positions that aims at the success of the client.

Managed Services

In this engagement model, Xove takes the full accountability of executing the projects/functions focused on managing SLAs and KPIs across projects, applications, infrastructure and IT operations. This model shifts the risk from Clientà Xove,, controls the cost and focus on core business goals. Other benefits includes getting the access to all Xove’s domain and technology experts, shielding client’s business from productivity loss and taking off the work load from clients staff. This model became popular now a days as the client will get the payback in the form of penalties if the outcome SLAs/KPIs are not met.

Managed Capacity

In this model, we allow the customer to buy a certain quantity of person-hours or person-days from Xove at a pre-negotiated Band/Tier wise rate. Client can use Xove’s services in all combinations that suits their specific needs and still be within budget.

This model is effective when the project outcome is more definite but the path to the closing end is prone to changes. If the nature of work, skills, or capabilities required to deliver the pre-decided outcome are likely to change, or if different kinds of skills are required at various phases of a multi-phase project but you’re not quite sure when, how, or how much, then this Model is very much suitable for you.

Fix.Bid

The fixed-price model works best for small projects with limited scope, clear requirements, no unknowns, time and effort required can be accurately estimated.

The expectations are agreed at the beginning of the engagement and there should be minimal/no changes throughout the engagement. This enables clients to tightly control the budget while availing the benefits of Xove’s Global talent pool, processes and tools. Transparency, Trust and continued communication are crucial for the successful execution of this engagement model.
